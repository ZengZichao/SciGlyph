## 01 Cells & Microbes: Morphology and Structure

Covers morphology, structure and arrangement of prokaryotic/eukaryotic cells and viruses. Subfolders follow the object of study: morphology & arrangement, cell structures, cell envelope, appendages & motility, archaea, viruses & phages, eukaryotic cells & organelles. Scientific conventions: bacterial morphology/arrangement follows standard textbooks; archaeal phylum names follow GTDB; virus shapes are schematic rather than EM-faithful.
